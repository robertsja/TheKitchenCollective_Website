About

Simple food done well, with everything taken care of.

Born out of a passion for food, a love of hosting parties, and the hatred of salmon blinis, The Kitchen Collective has catered many events for top companies, popular fashion brands and private clients over its four-year span.

The Kitchen Collective has a simple ethos: simple food done well, a collaborative approach with clients, seamless service, and a good drink to wash it all down.

Together, Kirsty and Andrew offer and manage full-service events, taking care of libations, gastronomy and everything in between.
